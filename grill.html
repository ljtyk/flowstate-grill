Flowstate Grill

One grill and a rail of tickets. Everything cooks to the beat, so after a few minutes you stop reading and start feeling it.

  1. 1Tap an ingredient to drop it on the grill.
  2. 2When the ring closes, tap it on the beat to flip. Tap again when it closes to plate.
  3. 3A crackle sounds one beat before it's ready. Headphones help.
  4. ★

    Every item has its own move. Burger: press on the beat after it lands, hold one beat, release to smash. Steak: after the flip, pull at the ticket's temp. Veg: turn it four times. Chicken: tap on the off-beat while it cooks to baste for a bigger tip.

  5. 4A plate waits on its ticket until the whole ticket is up, and it cools while it waits. Fire the slow items first so everything lands together. Plate something no ticket wants and it's binned at food cost.
  6. 5Nothing ends the service early. Burn something, tap to toss it, fire another.
